Studio Culture

The Upper Mall Drafting Room

Our studio operates as an architectural laboratory. We do not rely exclusively on computer monitors; physical timber scale models, raw plaster mockups, and hand-drafted charrettes remain central to our everyday design process.

Our material library houses hundreds of sourced samples: local Khushab sandstone, Roman travertine, fluted teak from Chiniot, and thermally broken aluminum extrusions—giving clients a tactile understanding of their future sanctuary.
