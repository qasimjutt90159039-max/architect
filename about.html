Established 2006 • Lahore, Pakistan

Crafting Enduring Architecture with Spatial Precision.

Headquartered at Scotch Corner on Lahore's historic Upper Mall corridor, Archtech has stood at the vanguard of Pakistani architectural discourse for nearly two decades.

The Story

Born in Lahore, Built for Generations.

Founded in 2006, Archtech emerged from a profound conviction: that contemporary architecture in Pakistan must not merely mimic foreign corporate trends, nor devolve into nostalgic pastiche. Instead, it must respond dynamically to the subtropical climate of Punjab, the rich masonry traditions of the subcontinent, and the demanding spatial aspirations of progressive families and institutions.

From our studio at 9 Scotch Corner, Upper Mall Scheme, we have directed commissions spanning over two million square feet of covered space—ranging from sculptural residences in Gulberg and DHA to corporate towers along Lahore's primary commercial arteries and institutional campuses in Islamabad and Multan.

Guiding Principles

Mission, Vision & Core Values

The bedrock convictions directing every structural calculation, elevation curve, and material specification.

Our Studio Mission

To engineer timeless, bioclimatically intelligent spaces that elevate human well-being, honor local craftsmanship, and set uncompromising structural benchmarks across South Asia.

Our Architectural Vision

To be recognized as Pakistan's preeminent architectural practice, renowned for harmonizing rigorous modernist minimalism with the climatic and cultural vernacular of the subcontinent.

Our Core Values

Integrity in materiality, radical transparency in budgetary allocations, deep environmental stewardship, and obsessive detailing that treats construction joints as poetic art.
