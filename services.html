Frequently Asked Questions

Practical details on building permits in Lahore, design fees, and project workflows.

As a fully registered firm with the Pakistan Council of Architects and Town Planners (PCATP), Archtech handles the entire vetting and submission pipeline. Our structural engineers and town planners prepare code-compliant architectural drawings that satisfy setback bylaws, height restrictions, covered area ratios, and seismic parameters for LDA, DHA Phase 1–9, and Lahore Cantt boards.

For a bespoke 1-kanal or 2-kanal residence, the complete architectural design package (including concept, 3D visualizations, structural calculations, and MEP drawings) generally spans 8 to 12 weeks. Large-scale corporate towers and institutional commissions usually range from 16 to 24 weeks, depending on client feedback cycles and municipal sanctioning windows.
